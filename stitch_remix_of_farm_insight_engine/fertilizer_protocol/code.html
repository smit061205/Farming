Why this timing?

The pre-sunrise window is critical for nitrogen absorption. During these hours, the stomata of the vine prepare for transpiration as the first light hits. Applying granular urea during the 4-6 AM window leverages the higher relative humidity and lower soil temperature, which prevents the volatilization of ammonia. This ensures that the nutrients are dissolved by the morning dew and move directly into the root zone just as the plant's metabolic activity peaks.
