# Color Scheme: Technological Terroir The color palette for the Technological Terroir platform is inspired by "The Digital Agrarian" north star—a fusion of ancestral earthiness and high-precision AI intelligence. ### 🌿 Base & Neutral Tones Used for primary backgrounds and large surface areas to provide a warm, organic foundation. - **Base Background (Cream):** `#fefae0` (Soft, paper-like cream that reduces eye strain and feels natural). - **Secondary Background:** `#f5f1da` (Used for subtle tonal layering to define sections without borders). - **Deep Slate/Stone:** `#1d1c0d` (Used in dark mode or for high-contrast secondary elements). ### 🌲 Primary Brand Colors The core identity of the platform, used for key actions, navigation, and emphasis. - **Forest Green (Primary):** `#173809` (A deep, lush green representing growth and stability). - **Lime Accent:** `#d1e8c4` (Used for highlight elements and subtle glowing indicators). ### 🏺 Accent & Emotional Tones Used sparingly for emotional impact, specialized sections, or brand flair. - **Terracotta:** `#9f402d` (An earthy, fired-clay red used for "Archive" views or subtle secondary call-outs). ### 💡 Status & Intelligence System Instead of flat colors, we use "glowing" versions of these tones to indicate system status. - **Healthy (Green Glow):** `#173809` with opacity/blur (Optimal field conditions). - **Warning (Yellow/Amber Glow):** `#f9c74f` (Needs attention, like low moisture). - **Critical (Red Glow):** `#e63946` (Urgent threats like frost or severe deficiency). ### 🔤 Typography & Contrast - **Primary Text:** `#173809` (Ensures high readability and brand consistency). - **Subtle Text:** `#173809` with 60% opacity (For metadata and descriptions). - **Light Text (on Dark):** `#fefae0` (Used on primary buttons or dark mode surfaces). --- **Design Rule:** NEVER use 1px borders. Always separate sections using these tonal variations (`#fefae0` vs `#f5f1da`) and soft, diffused shadows.
